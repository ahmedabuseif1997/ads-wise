Public Authority Data Request Policy

ARKS Investment Holding Ltd · Effective date: 4 November 2026

1. Purpose and scope

ARKS Investment Holding Ltd discloses Adswise user data to a public authority only when a request is lawful, verified, approved and limited to the minimum data required.

This policy covers all personal and business data held by Adswise, including Meta Platform Data and client workspace data; demands from government bodies, regulators, courts, law-enforcement and other public authorities in any country; and all staff, contractors and service providers who handle Adswise data.

No data is disclosed outside this process. Voluntary disclosure without a legal obligation is prohibited, except for a verified emergency involving imminent risk of death or serious injury.

2. Roles and responsibilities

  • Policy Owner: approves or refuses every request, signs off disclosures and owns this policy.
  • Legal Reviewer: external legal counsel checks legality, jurisdiction and scope where required.
  • Technical Lead: securely extracts only the approved data and logs the extraction.
  • All staff: forward requests within one business day without replying or sharing data.

All requests must be sent to info@ads-wise.com; requests are not handled informally.

3. Intake and verification

  1. Do not respond or disclose data. Forward the request to the Policy Owner within one business day.
  2. Log the request with a unique reference number on the day it reaches the Policy Owner.
  3. Independently confirm that the authority exists, the sender works there, and the request was issued through an official channel with a stated legal basis.
  4. Confirm that the request identifies the authority, legal basis, affected accounts or users, requested data, time period and deadline.
  5. Refuse and document any request that cannot be verified.

4. Legal review and challenge

The Legal Reviewer confirms that the authority has jurisdiction, the cited legal basis requires disclosure, the request is specific and proportionate, and disclosure would not breach another law, contract or applicable platform terms.

ARKS challenges requests issued without valid authority, outside the authority's jurisdiction, overly broad in scope, unclear, or conflicting with applicable law. Data remains held until the challenge is resolved.

Emergency requests: when a verified authority demonstrates an imminent risk of death or serious injury, the Policy Owner may approve a limited disclosure before full review. The request must be reviewed and logged within two business days.

5. Data minimisation and security

  • Disclose only the accounts, fields and date range legally required.
  • Never disclose access tokens, app secrets or other credentials.
  • Remove or redact data concerning unrelated users and workspaces.
  • Use aggregated or anonymised data whenever it satisfies the request.
  • Deliver approved data through an encrypted channel to a verified contact and retain an exact record of what was sent.

6. User notification

The affected workspace owner is notified before disclosure and told the requesting authority, requested data and planned response, unless a law or court order prohibits notice. When notice is prohibited, Adswise records the legal reason and notifies the client when the restriction ends. Emergency disclosures are notified after the fact as soon as legally permitted.

7. Documentation and retention

Every request, verification, legal decision, challenge, disclosure and notification is recorded in a restricted request register retained for at least five years. The register includes the reference number, authority, verified contact, legal basis, requested data and period, verification method, decision and reasoning, exact data disclosed, delivery method, client-notification status and approving Policy Owner.

Adswise received no public-authority requests in the 12 months ending 4 November 2026.

8. Service providers and Meta Platform Data

Service providers with access to Adswise data include Vercel (hosting), Neon PostgreSQL (encrypted credential and workspace storage), BytePlus/Volcengine ModelArk Seed (limited campaign configuration for pre-launch checks) and Resend (limited approval notifications). Requests received by a provider must be routed through this policy where legally permitted.

When a request covers Meta Platform Data, ARKS follows Meta's Platform Terms and notifies Meta where those terms require it.

9. Training and review

Relevant staff are briefed at onboarding and annually. The Policy Owner reviews this policy every 12 months and whenever applicable law, platform terms or service providers change.
